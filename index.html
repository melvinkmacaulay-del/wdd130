Whitewater Adventures

Our Purpose

We believe everyone deserves to experience the thrill and beauty of nature through whitewater rafting. Our mission is to provide safe, memorable, and life-changing adventures while respecting and protecting the rivers we love.

Our History

Founded in 1995 by a group of passionate river guides, Whitewater Adventures began with just two rafts on the Salmon River. Over the past three decades, we have grown into a respected rafting company known for our experienced guides, commitment to safety, and dedication to environmental stewardship.
