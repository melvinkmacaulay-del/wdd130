Our History

Founded in 1995, Whitewater Adventures began with a small raft and a big dream on the Salmon River. What started as weekend trips for friends has grown into one of the most respected rafting companies in the Pacific Northwest.

Over the past 30 years, we've guided thousands of adventurers through some of the most thrilling rapids in North America while maintaining an unwavering commitment to safety and environmental stewardship.
