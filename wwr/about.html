About Whitewater Adventures

30 years of guiding unforgettable river experiences

Our Purpose

We believe everyone deserves to experience the thrill and beauty of nature through whitewater rafting. Our mission is to provide safe, memorable, and life-changing adventures while respecting and protecting the rivers we love.

Every trip we run is guided by our three core values: Safety, Conservation, and Fun. We take great pride in equipping every guest with the skills, gear, and confidence they need before they ever hit the water.

Our History

Founded in 1995 by a group of passionate river guides, Whitewater Adventures began with just two rafts on the Salmon River. Over the past three decades, we have grown into a respected rafting company known for our experienced guides, commitment to safety, and dedication to environmental stewardship. We have introduced tens of thousands of guests to the power and beauty of North America's greatest rivers.
