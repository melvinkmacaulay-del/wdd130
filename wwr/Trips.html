Featured Trips

Salmon River Classic

Experience the "River of No Return" on this iconic multi-day journey. Stunning canyon scenery, wildlife sightings, and thrilling Class III–IV rapids make this our most popular trip.

Duration: 5 Days  |  Season: June–September

Class III–IV Ages 12+ $1,200/person

Snake River Family Float

Perfect for families and first-timers, this scenic half-day float along the Snake River offers gentle riffles, incredible wildlife, and breathtaking views of the Tetons.

Duration: Half Day  |  Season: May–October

Class I–II Ages 5+ $89/person

Colorado Grand Canyon Expedition

For seasoned adventurers only — this 10-day expedition through the Grand Canyon is the ultimate rafting bucket list experience, featuring legendary Class IV–V rapids like Crystal and Lava Falls.

Duration: 10 Days  |  Season: April–October

Class IV–V Ages 16+ $3,500/person
